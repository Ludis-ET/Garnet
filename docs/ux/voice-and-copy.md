# Voice and copy

Garnet sounds like a warm, slightly chaotic friend who adores you both (§6: "playful and chaotic but remains affectionate"). All copy here is **draft for review**.

## Rules

| Do                                                                            | Don't                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Invite: "Got a moment? Garnet's listening."                                   | Pressure: "You haven't shared today!"                        |
| Celebrate together: "7 days of sharing your days 💜"                          | Score: "Your connection score is 82%"                        |
| Neutral status: "Grace kept your Spark glowing."                              | Blame: "Sapphire didn't share yesterday."                    |
| Calm rest: "Your Spark is resting. It'll grow again whenever you both share." | Loss: "Your streak is broken / lost / dead."                 |
| Plain privacy: "Only you can see this draft."                                 | Vague privacy: "Some people may see this."                   |
| Honest delivery: "Sent"                                                       | Unknowable claims: "Seen", "Read", "Ignored"                 |
| Kind humour about the app or the distance                                     | Humour about either partner, their feelings or their absence |

**Banned words in Spark, widget and notification copy:** broken, lost, failed (about people), missed (as an accusation), ignored, seen, read, lazy, again?!, streak danger, last chance, don't let … down.

Character reactions are never sad, angry or abandoned (§2, §7). The worst a character does is nap.

## Widget nudges

Rotate in the **Calm** state. Privacy-neutral: no names, content, counts or status. Time-of-day sets are optional.

- Garnet is holding the crystal. Very carefully. Mostly.
- Today's chaos report: pending your input.
- A tiny moment counts. Even a snack. _Especially_ a snack.
- The crystal says hi. It's a crystal, so it said it sparkly.
- Somewhere, two gems are thinking about each other.
- Plot twist: today is a good day to share something small.
- Garnet is fully fused and ready to listen.
- Breaking news: your day is interesting. Sources: Garnet.
- Moments, like snacks, are better shared.
- Morning, gem. (morning) · Evening, gem. The day's still open. (evening)
